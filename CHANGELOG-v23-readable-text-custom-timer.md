# Day Flow v23 — readable text and custom focus timer

- Increased contrast of secondary text and placeholders across the app while retaining the night-sky glass palette.
- Replaced fixed 25/5/15-minute timer presets with user-defined work and break durations (1–180 minutes).
- Saved timer preferences locally so they persist on the same device.
- Kept task/habit selection, focus completion rewards, pause, reset, and ambient sound functionality.
- Timer settings can be applied before selecting either work or break mode.
